# SMARTIES — Content Strategy (GTM)

**Owner:** HERALD (CMO) · **Execution partner:** QUILL (VP Content) for calendars, voice, and production detail.  
**Product anchors:** zero tolerance for critical allergy misses; ~3s scan-to-result; clear red / yellow / green; privacy-by-design (local-first, minimal PII).

---

## Strategic frame

SMARTIES sits at the intersection of **safety-critical trust** and **consumer convenience**. Content must never outrun product truth: narratives that promise certainty before the app can back it up will damage the brand faster than silence. The primary job of early content is **credibility and clarity**, not volume. SEO matters for discoverability, but **community and clinical trust** convert for this category; optimize for both by leading with accuracy, sourcing, and humility (“always verify labels”).

---

## Blog / article series

**Use a blog — yes, but lean and purposeful.** A lightweight site or docs-adjacent “Safety & product” section is enough for MVP; a full content marketing engine is not required on day one.

| Role | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Trust** | Explain how matching works, limits (stale data, regional SKUs, reformulations), and what red/yellow/green mean. |
| **SEO** | Capture intent around “barcode allergy app,” “halal scan,” “ingredient checker” — always paired with medical/disclaimer framing. |
| **Support deflection** | One canonical article per high-friction topic reduces repeated explanations. |

**Suggested pillar topics (8–12 posts over the first 6–9 months, not a weekly grind):**

1. **North star:** Why “zero critical misses” drives product decisions (and what users should still double-check).
2. **How scanning works:** Barcode → data → profile rules → signal; offline behavior.
3. **Red / yellow / green:** Definitions, edge cases, and when to consult a professional.
4. **Privacy:** What stays on device, what does not, retention, export/delete — aligned with GDPR-style controls.
5. **Religious & medical diets:** Respectful, non-prescriptive explainers (halal/kosher/medical — partner-reviewed where possible).
6. **When the barcode fails:** Honest MVP boundary; roadmap tease (Phase 2) without overpromising.
7. **Accessibility:** VoiceOver/TalkBack, WCAG-minded UI as product values.
8. **Changelog / safety notices:** When logic or data changes affect outcomes, publish a short factual note.

**Cadence:** **Now:** 1–2 foundational pieces pre- or at soft launch. **Next:** 1 article / month + incident-driven updates. **Later:** Series depth (e.g. “family profiles,” retailer partnerships) as features ship.

**SEO vs community trust:** Pair every SEO-targeted article with visible **last reviewed** date, **limitations**, and **not medical advice** language. Prioritize backlinks and quotes from **recognized allergy advocates and clinicians** over keyword stuffing.

## Video

**When video helps:** Education that is hard to skim (scan flow, interpreting signals, privacy settings), **social proof** (short testimonials, not actors), and **demo** for press or partners.

**Formats (MVP-realistic):**

| Format | Production level | Use |
|--------|-------------------|-----|
| **Screen + voiceover** (60–120s) | Low — internal or freelancer | App walkthrough, “how to read your result.” |
| **Founder / PM explainer** | Low — webcam OK | Trust, mission, safety stance. |
| **Clinic or advocate 30s clip** | Medium — coordinate approvals | Credibility; use subtitles and plain language. |
| **Polished brand spot** | High — defer | Only after repeatable product story and legal comfort. |

**Now:** One **scan-to-result** screen recording with disclaimer overlay. **Next:** 2–3 shorts for social and help center. **Later:** Partner co-created education series if distribution is secured.

## Organic growth: partners, user groups, sequencing

**Partner types (prioritized for trust and reach):**

1. **Allergy advocates & nonprofits** — Align on safety messaging; co-develop disclaimers; consider advisory input on copy.
2. **Religious and cultural diet communities** — Leaders who can vouch for respectful framing; avoid tokenism; listen before publishing.
3. **Clinicians / dietitians (non-prescriptive)** — Quotes, review of educational articles, not endorsement of specific products unless compliant with their policies.
4. **Retailers / brands** — **Later** stage; requires stable data pipeline and legal clarity; start with informational relationships, not splashy co-marketing at MVP.

**User groups:** Parent communities, celiac/gluten groups, religious student orgs, accessibility communities. Lead with **listening** (surveys, beta feedback), then **invite** advocates to early access when the product is stable enough not to embarrass them.

**Sequencing vs product readiness:**

| Phase | Product gate | GTM emphasis |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **Now** | Alpha/beta with clear limits | Closed beta comms, founder narrative, 1–2 blog pillars, disclaimer-heavy demo video. No paid scale. |
| **Next** | MVP in store conditions, metrics on latency & accuracy | Public beta or launch, advocate outreach, monthly article, short video library, first case studies. |
| **Later** | Phase 2 reliability (e.g. barcode fallback), stronger analytics | SEO expansion, partnerships, localized content, retailer conversations. |

Do **not** mass outbound or influencer spend until **red/yellow/green** behavior and **privacy story** are stable and documented.

## Phased recommendations

### Now (0–3 months)

- Publish **safety + privacy + “how it works”** articles; single demo video with disclaimers.
- Identify **3–5** potential advocate contacts for relationship-only outreach (no public promises).
- Define **voice & review checklist** with QUILL (VP Content): medical caution, inclusive language, no fear-mongering.

### Next (3–9 months)

- Steady **monthly** article cadence; help-center parity with top support questions.
- **2–3** advocate or clinical quotes integrated into site; first **user story** (with consent).
- Light **social proof** on landing: metrics you can stand behind (e.g. scan latency under good conditions).

### Later (9+ months)

- **Partnership** pilots with aligned orgs; co-branded education only with legal review.
- **Localized** or community-specific guides as you have data coverage.
- Consider **long-form** SEO only when engineering and data quality support the claims.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Target / note |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trust proxies** | Advocate or clinical willingness to link or quote; low negative sentiment on safety threads. |
| **Content efficacy** | Help articles reduce repeat tickets; time-on-page on safety pages. |
| **Discovery** | Branded search growth; ranked snippets for 2–3 core non-brand queries (with honest titles). |
| **Activation** | Install → first successful scan funnel (content attributed via UTM or self-reported). |
| **Speed perception** | Messaging aligned with **~3s** reality; no creative that implies instant magic. |
| **Incident response** | Time to publish corrective comms if a content or product claim is challenged. |
